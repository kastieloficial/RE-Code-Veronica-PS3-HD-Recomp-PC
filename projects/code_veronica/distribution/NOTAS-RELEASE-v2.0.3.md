# v2.0.3 — Smooth 60 FPS in the tank room during the alarm / 60 FPS suave na sala dos tanques durante o alarme

## English

- **Smooth 60 FPS in the tank room (rm_1050) while the alarm flashes.** In this room, once the red alarm light starts flashing, the game marks the room with a special lighting flag, and the native 60 FPS smoothing switched itself off for the whole room: the character moved at 30 Hz even though the frame counter showed 60 (or 120 with frame generation). The smoothing now stays on in this room during the alarm. The exception applies **only to this room**; every other room, and the usual safeguards (Game Over, rifle and Linear Launcher scope, messages, events and room changes), behave exactly as before.
- **More shaders compiled before play:** 6,294 combinations (was 6,281).
- Includes everything from **v2.0.2** (paid Japanese PSN edition support, the game held completely on the shader screen, door skip credits). Applies to **EU, US and JP**.

Downloads: extract the complete **CodeVeronica-PC-v2.0.3.zip** and open **Code Veronica.exe**. Keep `bin/` and `assets/` alongside it. **Veronica-PKG-Extractor-v1.0.1.zip** is supplied separately and is unchanged. Supply your own original PS3 game files; no original game archives, 3D models, saves or personal settings are included.

## Português

- **60 FPS suave na sala dos tanques (rm_1050) com o alarme piscando.** Nessa sala, quando a luz vermelha do alarme começa a piscar, o jogo marca a sala com uma indicação de iluminação especial, e a suavização nativa de 60 FPS se desligava na sala inteira: o personagem andava a 30 Hz, mesmo com o contador mostrando 60 (ou 120 com geração de quadros). Agora a suavização continua ligada nessa sala durante o alarme. A exceção vale **só para essa sala**; todas as outras salas e as proteções de sempre (Game Over, mira do rifle e do Linear Launcher, mensagens, eventos e troca de sala) continuam exatamente como antes.
- **Mais shaders compilados antes de jogar:** 6.294 combinações (eram 6.281).
- Inclui tudo da **v2.0.2** (suporte à edição japonesa paga da PSN, jogo parado por completo na tela de shaders, créditos do pular portas). Vale para **EU, US e JP**.
