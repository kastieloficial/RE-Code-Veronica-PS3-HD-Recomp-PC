# v2.0.2 — Paid Japanese edition supported / Edição japonesa paga suportada

## English

- **The paid PSN edition of the Japanese version now works.** BIOHAZARD CODE: Veronica Kanzenban (NPJB00135) exists in two editions: a free-licence one and the paid PSN one, whose EBOOT.BIN needs your `.rap`, like the European and American versions. The launcher already decrypted the paid edition, but then refused it with "This EBOOT is not the compatible version". The game code of both editions is identical (only a 9-byte title text inside the game data differs), so the launcher now accepts both. Pick your game folder and your `.rap` on the Japan card, as with EU and US.
- **The decrypted EBOOT cache now remembers which EBOOT.BIN it came from.** Two folders of the same version (such as the two Japanese editions) no longer share a stale decrypted EBOOT; switching folders decrypts again once.
- Thanks to **curtiz** for reporting the issue on speedrun.com and helping to test it.
- Includes everything from **v2.0.1**. Applies to **EU, US and JP**.

Downloads: extract the complete **CodeVeronica-PC-v2.0.2.zip** and open **Code Veronica.exe**. Keep `bin/` and `assets/` alongside it. **Veronica-PKG-Extractor-v1.0.1.zip** is supplied separately and is unchanged. Supply your own original PS3 game files; no original game archives, 3D models, saves or personal settings are included.
